GTA 6 Trophies and Achievements: What the Platinum Takes

GTA 6 trophies explained: the 55-trophy breakdown, how long the Platinum takes, the hardest and missable ones, and a spoiler-free plan for 100%.

Updated Nov 24, 20266 min read6 sources checked
QUICK ANSWER

GTA 6 has 55 trophies on PS5 (1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze) and the same 55 as achievements worth 1,000 Gamerscore on Xbox Series X|S. The Platinum, Vice Got a Price!, needs every other trophy including 100% completion. PowerPyx rates it 4 out of 10 for difficulty and about 200 hours, and lists no online or missable trophies.

How many trophies are in GTA 6?

Grand Theft Auto VI launched with 55 trophies on PS5, and the Xbox Series X|S version mirrors them as 55 achievements worth 1,000 Gamerscore. On PlayStation the capstone is the Platinum, Vice Got a Price!, which you earn by unlocking everything else; Xbox has an equivalent completion achievement.

There are no online or DLC trophies at launch, so the whole list is single-player. Just over half of it (28 of 55) is hidden, mostly story trophies, while the visible ones cover activities, collectibles, robberies and stunts across Leonida.

How long does the GTA 6 Platinum take, and is it hard?

Trophy guide site PowerPyx rates the Platinum 4 out of 10 for difficulty and estimates roughly 200 hours. Nothing demands elite skill, and the game has no difficulty settings, so the challenge is volume: hundreds of stunts, dozens of medals and a long 100% checklist. One playthrough is enough.

Story length estimates vary. PowerPyx budgets 60 to 80 hours for the campaign plus optional missions and scanning tasks, while other reports put the main story alone anywhere from 50 to around 80 hours. TheGamer reported on November 23 that the first Platinum tracked on PSNProfiles belonged to a player named Danpool85, who had been playing for over a week; TheGamer suspects an early review copy.

Are there missable trophies in GTA 6?

Effectively, no. PowerPyx lists zero missable and zero glitched trophies. Story trophies unlock automatically as you play, and after the final mission you stay in free roam with everything needed for the 100% checklist still available. There is no mission replay, but no trophy depends on one.

The one trophy some guides flag is Positive Mental Attitude. During Chapter 1, Lucia offers to show Jason how to meditate at his house, and that moment can't be repeated. KeenGamer and GGRecon call it possibly missable, while PowerPyx says only the open-world meditation spots marked with a purple icon count.

Tip

Do the Chapter 1 meditation anyway. It takes a minute and removes the only doubt on the list.

Quick one-off trophies worth grabbing early

These take minutes once you know what to do, and a few of them are hidden on the list. Requirements come from PowerPyx and KeenGamer.

  • Challenging Tank (hidden): steal a Rhino tank. PowerPyx says tanks sit at Mile Zero Air Station on the south-west island in Mariana County; climb in and shoot some enemies until it pops.
  • Catch Me If You Can (hidden): steal one of the big passenger jets parked at a departure gate at Vice City International Airport. The unlock lags until you are about 1 km (0.6 miles) away.
  • Personal Best (hidden): max out Strength on Jason and Lucia at the same time.
  • Hot Together: escape a four-star wanted level with your partner.
  • Retail Therapy: rob three shops back to back without shaking your wanted level in between.
  • Gunslinger: take down five enemies during a single use of Combat Focus.
  • Master of None: try all nine activity types once: fishing, hunting, golf, a shooting range, pool, basketball, a video game, a race win and a stunt.
  • Little Boxes: buy all eight purchasable garages, about $160,000 in total. Story garages and the collectible garage don't count.

Story and questline trophies (spoiler-free)

Light story spoilers — tap to reveal

Twelve trophies come from the main story: chapter completions, key missions and a Gold for the finale. They unlock on their own and can't be missed, so we are not listing their names, several of which hint at plot beats.

Five more Bronze trophies reward finishing the questlines of side characters Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, and Fair Game asks you to hunt a unique animal for Bret. Don't confuse them with Strangers & Freaks: PowerPyx notes that big questlines such as Andy's, Billy's and Tyrone's don't count toward its eight stranger strands.

GTA 6 Platinum roadmap: tips for 100%

  1. Play the story first and take optional missions as they pop up. Story trophies unlock by themselves.
  2. Scan vehicles and study animals as you travel. The checklist wants 200 of 277 base vehicle models and 100 of 255 animals, and you don't need to hunt or skin anything for the study count. Whip It Good (100 vehicles) comes along the way.
  3. After the credits, open the 100% checklist under Progress, then Stats. Robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's quests must be fully done; most other categories only need a share.
  4. Clear 70 of the 80 challenges for the checklist. One Last Thing pops at 50.
  5. Hit all eight big bank jobs and all 39 gang operations. One operation per gang is enough for Pest Control, but the checklist wants every one.
  6. Launder marked bills through a fence as you go. Dime on the Dollar counts $250,000 across all your robberies.
  7. Finish with Strangers & Freaks and any leftover one-off trophies.
Tip

The in-game 100% is not a true 100%. PowerPyx puts it closer to 60 percent of everything in the game, so don't chase every last collectible.

Do cheats disable trophies in GTA 6?

Yes. Entering a cheat turns off trophy unlocks and also disables saving, according to PowerPyx and KeenGamer, which adds that the block applies to your current session. Keep cheat experiments away from your trophy run so you don't lose progress you meant to save.

GTA 6 trophies FAQ

How many trophies does GTA 6 have?

55 on PS5: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. Xbox Series X|S has the same 55 as achievements worth 1,000 Gamerscore.

What is the GTA 6 Platinum trophy called?

Vice Got a Price! It unlocks once you have earned every other trophy in the game.

How long does it take to platinum GTA 6?

About 200 hours, according to PowerPyx, which also rates it 4 out of 10 for difficulty. The story and early side content alone take roughly 60 to 80 hours.

Does GTA 6 have missable trophies?

PowerPyx lists none, because everything needed for 100% stays available in free roam after the story. Some guides flag the one-time Chapter 1 meditation for Positive Mental Attitude, so do it to be safe.

Are there online trophies in GTA 6?

No. All 55 trophies are single-player, and GTA 6 has no online mode at launch.

What is the hardest trophy in GTA 6?

A Long Road, the Gold for reaching 100% on the in-game checklist, takes the longest. Cunning Stunts (250 stunts) and The Shootist (all 42 shooting range golds) are the next biggest grinds.

Do cheats disable trophies in GTA 6?

Yes. Using cheats disables trophies and saving, so keep them away from your trophy run.
