GTA 6 Collectibles Guide: What to Find, What You Get and How to Track It

GTA 6 collectibles explained: cards, WAiNK scans, cocaine flamingos and more, with counts, rewards, 100% requirements and how to track your progress.

Updated Nov 24, 20267 min read27 sources checked
QUICK ANSWER

GTA 6 logs its collectibles in the Progress menu: open the map, switch to Progress and choose Collections. The big hunts are 18 collectible card sets, WAiNK phone scans (100 earn a trophy), 125 cocaine flamingos, 33 underwater discoveries, meditation spots and reading material. Rewards include trophies, cash, post-story cheats and even a few free cars.

What collectibles are in GTA 6?

GTA 6 doesn't stop at one collectible hunt. Its Progress menu keeps a huge database of things to find, scan, read and study, and guide site PowerPyx, which mirrors that menu, counts around 3,000 entries once vehicles, weapons and animals are included.

The totals in the table come from the in-game Progress menu as logged by PowerPyx. GamesRadar, GameSpot and GameRant report matching numbers for animals, card sets, WAiNK landmarks, underwater discoveries and No Flusher tags.

How to track collectibles in GTA 6

Sub-categories only appear after you find at least one item, so a missing tab doesn't mean a collectible type doesn't exist.

  1. Open the map (touchpad on PS5), then press R1/RB to reach the Progress tab.
  2. Choose Collections for cards, WAiNK locations, murals, street art and the Exploration group, which holds flamingos, meditations, reading material, underwater finds and playlists.
  3. Check the Animals and vehicle lists for your discovery logs.
  4. After the story, look under Stats in the Progress tab for the full 100% Completion checklist.
Tip

Books and scanned posters don't show which ones you've already logged, so keep a quick checklist on your phone.

How to find collectibles faster

GTA 6 gives you plenty of built-in help. Watch for these signals:

  • Feel for the rumble. A sudden controller vibration, a sound cue and an on-screen notice mean a WAiNK target is close. Hold Up on the D-pad to raise your phone, then scan with R1/RB.
  • Use focus mode for cards. It highlights them, and a yellow card icon appears when one is within reach.
  • Watch for purple map icons. Meditation spots get marked as you approach them.
  • Sweep the metal detector underwater. A green flash points the way, and the pings speed up as you close in.
  • Study animals through binoculars. No hunting needed; just hold the study prompt until the meter fills.
  • Scan cars as you go. The WAiNK scan also reveals a vehicle's value, security features and registration cost.

What do you get for GTA 6 collectibles?

Rewards fall into five buckets:

  • Trophies and achievements: Don't Touch My Deck (complete 3 card sets), You WAiNKer! (scan 100 WAiNK locations), Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot), Whip It Good (discover 100 vehicles) and A Long Road (100% completion).
  • Cash: pawn shops buy cards, a fence will take flamingo cocaine, and Kenton pays for sunken treasure.
  • Post-story cheats: 50 cocaine flamingos unlock the Inflatable Flamingo, 10 and 20 watches unlock time-of-day cheats, all 24 Vice City Flamingos cards unlock the Bubblegum filter, and $250,000 from selling collectibles unlocks Pixel Art.
  • Vehicles: all five 1010 murals earn a 1010 Cheetah plus a garage, and GameRant reports that scanning 10 of the 15 No Flusher tags in Vice Beach starts a secret chain ending in a free Thrax supercar.
  • Map knowledge: some magazines mark hunting, fishing and other points of interest on your map.
Tip

Cheats only work after the main story, and using one switches off saving and trophies/achievements for that session, so keep a clean save.

GTA 6 collectible cards: sell or keep?

The 18 sets split into ten sports-team sets from Vice City, Liberty City and Los Santos, three dancer sets, three comic sets and two novelty sets. Cards turn up in public spaces, homes, robbable stores and missions across all five counties.

Any pawn shop will buy them, and prices depend on rarity. GameRant got $20 for a common card and about $6,000 for a rare one picked up in South Vice-Dale County, just north of Key Lento.

We haven't seen a reliable source confirm whether a sold card still counts toward its set. If you want the Don't Touch My Deck trophy or the Bubblegum filter, finish those sets before you sell from them.

WAiNK scans and cocaine flamingos explained

WAiNK is the scanner app on Jason and Lucia's phones. Its five sets hold 31 landmarks, 35 curiosities, 23 sculptures, 4 Peewees billboards and 15 No Flusher tags, and scanning 100 of them earns the You WAiNKer! trophy. Ahol Street Art and the 1010 murals are scanned the same way but tracked separately, so they don't count toward that 100.

Easy early scans sit in Key Lento: the multicolored Wonder Whale statue, the Badger R&D radio tower south of Brian's marina and a Christmas-decorated house on the westernmost street. Several curiosities are alien-looking carcasses with a meteorite pickup nearby, including one on Abismo Key.

Cocaine flamingos are pink flamingo statues with drug packages hidden inside. Only ones with black-and-white beaks count; black-and-yellow beaks are just decoration. Shoot one to break it open and log it. Confirmed early spots include Belpoint and a Watson Bay motel in Mariana County, plus La Perle in Vice City.

Missable GTA 6 collectibles: what to grab early

PowerPyx reports that nothing needed for 100% completion is permanently missable, because post-story free roam lets you finish the rest. A few individual spots still disappear, so handle these early:

  • Read every book and magazine in Jason's house before Chapter 1 ends, and do the same in the safehouses you get in Chapters 2 and 3. Those rooms close behind you, though most titles also appear elsewhere.
  • Say yes when Lucia suggests meditating early in the story. PowerPyx says that one-time scene isn't needed for the trophy, but GamesRadar flags it as potentially missable.
  • Start Kenton's diving jobs in Chapter 1. They pay well early, and all 33 underwater discoveries count toward 100%.
  • Scan vehicles and study animals from day one, since the checklist wants 200 base-model vehicles and 100 animals.

Which collectibles count toward 100% completion?

The A Long Road trophy needs 100% completion, and PowerPyx calls the checklist lenient, closer to 60% of everything in the game. Its collectible requirements:

GTA 6 collectibles guide FAQ

How many collectibles are in GTA 6?

It depends on what you count. The Progress menu holds around 3,000 entries including vehicles and animals, while core collections include 480 cards, 108 WAiNK scans and 125 cocaine flamingos.

Where can I see my collectibles in GTA 6?

Open the map, switch to the Progress tab and choose Collections. Animals and vehicles have their own discovery lists, and the 100% checklist appears under Stats after the story.

Should I sell collectible cards in GTA 6?

Selling is a quick source of cash, and rare cards can fetch thousands at pawn shops. Keep cards from any set you're trying to finish, since it isn't confirmed whether sold cards still count.

What do cocaine flamingos do in GTA 6?

Each one hides a cocaine package you can sell to a fence. Destroying 50 unlocks the Inflatable Flamingo cheat after the story, and 100 of the 125 count toward 100% completion.

How many WAiNK locations do you need in GTA 6?

You need 100. The You WAiNKer! trophy and the 100% checklist both ask for 100 scans from the WAiNK Locations sets, which hold 108 in total.

Are there missable collectibles in GTA 6?

Some individual spots, like reading material in early safehouses and a one-time meditation with Lucia, can't be revisited. PowerPyx says nothing needed for 100% is permanently missable.

Do collectibles unlock cheats in GTA 6?

Yes, but only after you finish the story. Examples include the Inflatable Flamingo for 50 cocaine flamingos and two time-of-day cheats for finding 10 and 20 collectible watches.

Is there a GTA 6 collectibles map?

Rockstar's official Leonida map poster doesn't mark collectibles. In the game, the Index's Exploration tab lists discovered points of interest, and guide sites are still mapping full locations.
