How to enter cheats in GTA 6 on PS5 and Xbox

Every code uses only the D-pad, so the inputs are identical on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Only the bumper names change.

  1. Finish the main story, then open the pause menu, go to the Progress tab and scroll right to Cheats. Locked cheats show a padlock, and highlighting one shows its requirement.
  2. In free roam, press Up on the D-pad to pull out your phone.
  3. Hold L1 on PS5 (LB on Xbox), then press and keep holding R1 (RB).
  4. With both bumpers held, enter the arrow sequence on the D-pad. If nothing happens, confirm the cheat is unlocked on this save and enter it again slowly.
Tip

There are no phone numbers to dial in GTA 6. Every confirmed cheat is a D-pad sequence entered with the phone out.

Do cheats disable trophies, achievements and saving?

Yes, but only for the current session. As soon as you enter any code, the game stops saving and stops awarding trophies and achievements. Nothing is taken away from your save file: quit, reload, and both work again.

The catch is that anything you do after cheating is lost when you quit, including the $250,000 from the money cheat. You can blow it on cars and guns for a sandbox session, but you can't keep it. A safe routine:

  1. Make a manual save before you touch a code.
  2. Enter your cheats and play around.
  3. Quit and reload that save to get saving, trophies and achievements back.

Fake GTA 6 cheats: how to spot them

  • Codes that use face buttons or phone numbers. Every confirmed GTA 6 cheat is D-pad only.
  • Anything that claims to work before the credits. PC Gamer and GameSpot both tested this, and codes do nothing on an unfinished save.
  • Infinite money cheats or glitches. The only money cheat gives $250,000, you can't save afterward, and Dexerto found no confirmed money glitch.
  • Lists with an extra or missing arrow. While checking sources we found four codes that at least one site printed with a wrong or missing press, so we only list sequences that match across major outlets.
  • Apps, mod menus or videos promising to skip the story requirement. Stick to the in-game method.

What to do while your cheats are locked

  • Chip away at unlock challenges during the story. Workouts, shooting range medals, stunt jumps and collectible watches all count once the credits roll.
  • Earn real money. Robberies and car flipping pay for everything the money cheat would, and that cash stays on your save. Our money guide covers the best methods by chapter.
  • Keep a separate story-complete save just for cheat sessions, so your main file is never at risk.
  • Hunt down rare vehicles instead of spawning them. Our secret pages cover a few you can grab early.

GTA 6 cheats FAQ

Does GTA 6 have cheats?

Yes. GTA 6 has 61 cheats across player, combat, vehicle spawn, environment and screen filter categories. They only work after you finish the main story, and each one needs its own challenge.

How do you enter cheats in GTA 6?

Press Up on the D-pad to pull out your phone, hold L1 (LB on Xbox), press and hold R1 (RB), then enter the D-pad code. The cheat must already be unlocked in the Cheats list of the Progress menu.

What is the money cheat in GTA 6?

Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right gives you $250,000. It unlocks when you finish the story, but cheats disable saving, so the money is gone when you quit.

Do GTA 6 cheats disable trophies and achievements?

Yes, for the current session only. Cheats also switch off saving, so quit and reload a save to earn trophies and achievements again.

Can you use cheats before beating GTA 6?

No. PC Gamer and GameSpot both tried codes on unfinished saves and they did nothing. Challenge progress you make during the story still counts toward unlocks.

Do cheat unlocks carry over to a new game?

No. Shacknews reports that a new save starts with cheats locked again, so you have to finish the story on each playthrough.

Is there a tank cheat in GTA 6?

Yes. The Hauners Rhino Tank spawn cheat unlocks after you complete all survival challenges, but its code had not been published as of November 24, 2026.

Are there GTA 6 cheats for PC?

Not yet. GTA 6 launched on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and Rockstar had not announced a PC version as of late November 2026.
