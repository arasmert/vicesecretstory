GTA 6 Map Guide: Leonida's Regions, Real Size and How to Get Around

The GTA 6 map explained: Leonida's five counties and six regions, how its size compares with GTA 5, key landmarks and the fastest ways to get around.

Updated Nov 24, 20267 min read22 sources checked
QUICK ANSWER

GTA 6 takes place in Leonida, Rockstar's fictional Florida. The official map splits the state into five counties (Vice-Dale, Mariana, Kelly, Leonard and Lummox), and Rockstar spotlights six regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. Rockstar says Leonida is roughly twice the size of GTA 5's map.

Where is GTA 6 set?

Everything happens in the state of Leonida, Rockstar's satirical take on Florida. Vice City fills the role of Miami, the Grassrivers wetlands echo the Everglades, and the Leonida Keys are modeled on the Florida Keys.

On launch day Rockstar added an official Leonida map poster to the media page of its GTA VI website as a free printable download. A double-sided physical copy was promised for the Rockstar Store at $0.99, or free with any store order, but Rockstar hasn't mentioned a paper map in the game's box.

Tip

Keep the official poster open on a second screen. It labels counties, towns, islands and highways, so the in-game map gets much easier to read.

GTA 6 map regions and counties

There are two ways to slice Leonida. Rockstar's official poster divides the state into five counties: Lummox in the north, Kelly in the west, Leonard across the middle and east coast, Vice-Dale around Vice City in the southeast, and Mariana covering the southwestern swamps and the southern islands.

Rockstar's GTA VI website promotes six headline regions, each inside one of those counties. The table shows how they line up, with landmarks to look for in each.

The official map also names smaller towns you'll pass through, such as upscale Capri on the west coast, Hamlet and its Boone Speedway between the Grassrivers and Vice City, Waning Sands and Belville north of Vice City, and Bonanza, Grayson and Millport in the far north.

How big is the GTA 6 map compared to GTA 5?

Rockstar's answer is about twice as big. In Game Informer's cover story, Rockstar North co-studio head Aaron Garbut called Leonida comfortably the largest world the studio has built and put it at roughly double GTA 5's map. Rockstar later told Edge that Leonida's total landmass, Vice City included, comes close to the playable maps of GTA 4, GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 added together.

Rockstar hasn't published an official size in square miles or kilometers, so exact figures online are fan estimates. Kotaku instead timed real trips from the southern tip to the far north. Its running time is slower partly because Jason and Lucia have a stamina bar.

Distance isn't everything, either: Garbut says Leonida has "an order of magnitude more interiors" than older GTA games.

Is the whole GTA 6 map unlocked from the start?

Mostly, yes. After the first mission of Chapter 1 you can open the full map, but it starts almost entirely blacked out, with only rough borders. The darkness lifts as you explore, and GamesRadar still had dark patches after about 80 hours.

We haven't seen any reliable report of story-locked regions. Once you reach Jason's home in Key Lento you can take his truck and head north; GamesRadar drove up the east coast into Vice City early on.

The story won't show you everything. Rockstar's narrative chief Rupert Humphries told Game Informer the campaign can't take you everywhere, and exploring all of Leonida earns its own trophy, The Goodtime State.

Tip

Drive new roads early. Exploring lifts the fog and adds shops and activities to your map.

Chapter 1 keeps you in Mariana County around Jason's place in Key Lento. Chapter 2 moves you to a motel safehouse near Port Gellhorn in Kelly County. In Chapter 3 you settle into an apartment in Vice City.

Does GTA 6 have fast travel?

Not in the click-and-teleport sense. You pay for a ride, take public transit or let your character drive:

  • Paradise Taxi: you get the number early in Chapter 1. Call from your phone's Contacts, pay cash, and focus on the driver (L2/LT) to skip the ride for an extra $100.
  • RydeMe: a ride-hailing app from Chapter 2 that bills your BuckMe bank account, shows the fare upfront and offers the same $100 skip. BuckMe Premium (Chapter 3) takes 20 percent off.
  • Buses and trains: board at a stop or station, pick a destination on the line and hold Triangle/Y to skip the trip. Cheaper, but limited to the route.
  • Auto-drive: in a vehicle, hold Down on the D-pad, press Options/Start for Cinematic Mode, then hold Circle/B. With a waypoint set, your character drives there at one of three speeds, even in boats.
  • Character switching: later in the story, swapping to your partner puts you wherever they are, often near a safehouse.
  • Scooter Bros: this app delivers your personal vehicle for a fee; the $12,500 Premium tier includes three free deliveries per in-game day.
  • Ferries: terminals are listed under Vehicles in the map Index.
Tip

Cash is tight early, so save the $100 skips for emergencies. Auto-drive costs nothing and doubles as a sightseeing tour.

How to use the GTA 6 map and Index

Button prompts below are for PS5. The D-pad inputs work the same way on Xbox.

  1. Press the touchpad to open the map, which shows every store, activity and mission you've discovered.
  2. Press Right on the D-pad to open the Index: Personal, Story, Robberies, Activities, Exploration, Vehicles, Weapons, Style and Food.
  3. Highlight a task or place and press X to set it as your one active destination.
  4. Press Square anywhere on the map to drop extra markers.
  5. Out in the world, hold Down on the D-pad to slow time and show nearby points of interest, then press R3 on one to route your GPS there.
  6. Orange diamonds in the Index mark anything newly discovered.

What happens at the edge of the map?

Leonida has hard limits. GameSpot found that flying past the boundary makes your aircraft run dry and crash, while a jet ski runs out of fuel and leaves you stranded. A streamer also met a dog on a jet ski far offshore, but GameSpot couldn't reproduce it, so treat that as a random event.

GTA 6 map guide FAQ

Is the GTA 6 map bigger than GTA 5?

Yes. Rockstar's Aaron Garbut told Game Informer that Leonida is about double the size of GTA 5's map and Rockstar's largest world yet.

Is the GTA 6 map bigger than Red Dead Redemption 2?

Yes. Rockstar told Edge that Leonida's landmass nearly matches the playable maps of GTA 4, GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 put together.

How big is the GTA 6 map in square miles?

Rockstar hasn't released an official figure in square miles or kilometers. Numbers online are fan estimates, so Rockstar's comparison with GTA 5 is the safest guide.

How many counties are in GTA 6?

Five. Rockstar's official Leonida map shows Lummox, Kelly, Leonard, Vice-Dale and Mariana counties.

Is GTA 6 based on Florida?

Yes. Leonida is Rockstar's fictional Florida, with Vice City standing in for Miami and Grassrivers for the Everglades.

How long does it take to drive across the GTA 6 map?

Kotaku drove from the southernmost road to the far north in about 11 minutes at full speed. A prop plane took just under six minutes.

Can you go to Vice City right away in GTA 6?

Pretty early, yes. Once you reach Jason's home in Key Lento in Chapter 1 you can drive north, and GamesRadar made it to Vice City that way.

Where can I get the official GTA 6 map poster?

Rockstar posted a free printable Leonida map poster on its GTA VI website's media page at launch. It also promised a double-sided physical copy on the Rockstar Store for $0.99, or free with any store purchase.
