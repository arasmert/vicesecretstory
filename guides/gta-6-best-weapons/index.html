GTA 6 Best Weapons: Top Picks by Class and How to Get Them Early

GTA 6 best weapons by class, free early guns, how the new weapon wheel and two-longarm limit work, and Ammu-Nation tips for keeping the guns you find.

Updated Nov 24, 20267 min read23 sources checked
QUICK ANSWER

GameSpot's top picks for GTA 6 are the Girardi ES9 pistol, the Duke 556 assault rifle, the Girardi AZ1200 or Shrewsbury Drifter shotgun, and the Fairview FF-50 sniper rifle. You can only carry two sidearms and two longarms at once, so keep extra guns in your personal vehicle's trunk or an Ammu-Nation weapon locker. Early on, attachments like a suppressor are a better buy than more guns.

Best weapons in GTA 6 by type

These picks are GameSpot's, based on its time with the standard guns sold at Ammu-Nation. Unique variants found in the world are often stronger, so swap one in when you get it.

Sidearms don't show on your character, so there's no downside to always keeping a pistol equipped. No major outlet has ranked heavy or melee weapons yet.

How to get good weapons early

You don't need a fortune to arm up. These sources are confirmed by hands-on guides, roughly in the order you can reach them:

  1. Smuggler Gorse 425 shotgun: free from the start in a smuggling boat on an island in the northern Permit Keys, near where Jason begins (TheGamer).
  2. Gear Up, Chapter 1: your first gun shop is Chuck's Tackle & Shot. GamesRadar suggests the Fortune & Son Moreland 850 shotgun for $350; the Duke 556 costs $1,450 and may use up your whole budget.
  3. Ultimate Edition: open any Ammu-Nation weapon locker as Jason in Chapter 1 to claim a personalized Girardi ES9. Lucia's Klose K17 and the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers follow in Chapter 2.
  4. Assassin Nipper .38: a silenced pistol hidden in the Derelict Spy Vessel wreck. Game Rant took a helicopter from the Southwind helipad east to the farthest island in the chain.
  5. Kingfishers W-22 rifle: comes with a red dot and suppressor fitted. It sits in the Kingfisher gang's fishing shacks in Wetman Bay, reachable by boat once a few story missions open the area (TheGamer).
  6. Police cars: pickups like the Bravado Bison have an unalarmed crate with guns and ammo. With the Slim Jim from Chapter 2, opening a cruiser's door also unlocks its trunk, which can hold an assault rifle, shotgun or body armor (PC Gamer).
  7. Final Chapter KVGA-12: a standout semi-auto shotgun in the Final Chapter biker gang's hideout in western Ambrosia, reported by both GameSpot and Game Rant.
Tip

Guns you loot from enemies or pick up in the world are unowned and vanish if you're Wasted or Busted. Unique variants like the ones above are the exception: they stay unlocked for good.

How the GTA 6 weapon wheel works

GTA 6's weapon wheel is now a loadout with fixed slots. Hold L1 on PS5 or LB on Xbox, on foot or in a vehicle, to open it. It has Weapons and Items tabs that you switch with R1/RB; highlight a slot with the right stick and press X/A to equip.

The Weapons tab has two sidearm slots, two longarm slots, and one slot each for melee, explosives, equipment such as binoculars or a flashlight, and your bare fists. The Items tab covers masks, headgear, headwear, outfits, bags, gear, eyewear and smokes.

How many weapons can you carry in GTA 6?

Two sidearms, two longarms, one melee weapon and one explosive. Sidearms include pistols, revolvers and compact SMGs; longarms cover rifles, shotguns, larger SMGs and snipers. GameSpot confirms there's no way to raise this cap, so storage does the rest.

Your personal vehicle's trunk is a mobile armory: press Triangle/Y to open it, hold L1/LB to swap gear, and press Square/X to stow longarms. Use a car you own, because guns left in a random car stay with that car. Owned guns also live in the green weapon lockers at most Ammu-Nation stores and in garages that have one.

Tip

Openly carried guns draw attention, and GamesRadar notes even Ammu-Nation staff don't like you waving a rifle around, so stow longarms when you don't need them.

How to keep the guns you find

GTA 6 sorts guns into three groups, according to GamesRadar:

  • Owned weapons: anything bought at Ammu-Nation or gifted in a mission. One purchase gives a copy to both Jason and Lucia, though each modifies and equips it separately. Lost owned guns can be recovered from your trunk or a weapon locker.
  • Unowned weapons: guns dropped by enemies or found lying around. They can't be modified or recovered, and they're gone if you're Wasted or Busted.
  • Unique Weapon Variants: named versions marked by a golden gun icon, usually found at special spots and illegal operation hideouts. Once picked up they're unlocked for both characters for good, but they can't be modified.
Tip

Missing a gun? At a weapon locker, choose Manage Weapons, pick the class and press Triangle/Y on a weapon to open its Variants tab, where lost guns usually turn up (GameSpot). Dropping an owned gun can also help you shake a Wanted level, since you can get it back later.

Ammu-Nation tips: stores, mods and ammo

PowerPyx has mapped 24 Ammu-Nation stores. Hover over a store's map icon to see its stock level: Limited, Standard, Hunting or Expanded. Expanded stores carry the widest range, including LMGs, grenade launchers and rocket launchers. Buying more guns anywhere gradually expands what the stores stock (GamesRadar).

Put early money into attachments. GameSpot argues a suppressor for an early assault rifle does more than buying three new rifles, and rates suppressors, magazines, foregrips, barrels and optics as strong buys. Ammu-Nation also sells ammo, body armor and parachutes, and GTA+ members get 10% off weapons at participating stores.

For free ammo, raid police pickup crates or pry open sealed wooden crates once you get the Pry Bar in Chapter 3. After you beat the story, the Max Ammo and Infinite Ammo cheats unlock through shooting range medals (GamesRadar).

GTA 6 best weapons FAQ

What is the best gun in GTA 6?

There's no single winner, but GameSpot's best all-rounder is the Duke 556 assault rifle. Pair it with a Girardi ES9 pistol and either a Girardi AZ1200 shotgun or a Fairview FF-50 sniper rifle.

How many weapons can you carry in GTA 6?

Two sidearms, two longarms, one melee weapon and one explosive. Extra guns go in your personal vehicle's trunk or an Ammu-Nation weapon locker.

How do you open the weapon wheel in GTA 6?

Hold L1 on PS5 or LB on Xbox to open the loadout wheel, then press R1/RB to switch between the Weapons and Items tabs.

Why did my gun disappear in GTA 6?

Guns you pick up from enemies or the world are unowned and are lost if you're Wasted or Busted. Buy the model at Ammu-Nation or stow owned guns in your personal vehicle, then check a weapon locker's Variants tab for anything missing.

Where do you buy guns in GTA 6?

At Ammu-Nation, which has 24 stores according to PowerPyx. Your first visit is Chuck's Tackle & Shot in the Chapter 1 mission Gear Up, and Expanded stores stock heavy weapons like LMGs and launchers.

How many guns are in GTA 6?

Reports vary. GameSpot counts 34 standard guns, while PowerPyx says the in-game tracker counts 66 standard-issue weapons including melee and explosives, and unique variants add many more.

Are there weapon cheats in GTA 6?

Yes, but only after you finish the story, and each cheat then needs its own challenge. GamesRadar lists Max Ammo as unlocked by winning 25 bronze or better medals at shooting ranges.

What are legendary weapons in GTA 6?

It's the community name for Unique Weapon Variants: named versions of standard guns found at special spots and hideouts, marked with a golden gun icon. They stay unlocked once found but can't be modified.
