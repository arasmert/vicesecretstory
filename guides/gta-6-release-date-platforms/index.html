GTA 6 PC release date, consoles, price and editions: what's confirmed

GTA 6 PC release date status, plus every platform, price, edition, download size and PS5 Pro detail confirmed since the November 19, 2026 launch.

Updated Nov 24, 20266 min read22 sources checked
QUICK ANSWER

GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Rockstar still hasn't announced a PC version or date, there's no PS4 version, and Xbox One owners can only stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming. The Standard Edition costs $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition $99.99.

When did GTA 6 come out?

Grand Theft Auto VI launched worldwide on Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It unlocked at midnight, with the exact time depending on your region and store.

It took two delays to get there. Rockstar originally aimed for 2025, then moved the game to May 26, 2026, and finally to November 19, 2026. The wait paid off with critics: Al Jazeera reported a Metacritic score of 99 from more than 150 reviews on launch day.

What platforms is GTA 6 on?

GTA 6 is only sold for current-generation consoles, though streaming opens a couple of side doors. The key rules come first, then the full platform-by-platform breakdown:

  • GTA 6 is not part of Xbox Game Pass. Microsoft's FAQ says you have to buy it; Game Pass members can only use their cloud hours to stream a copy they own.
  • Xbox Cloud Gaming for GTA 6 works on Xbox consoles only, not on phones, TVs or PCs.
  • Remote Play is supported on PlayStation and Xbox, and Sony's listing says cloud streaming to PS5 and PlayStation Portal requires PlayStation Plus Premium.
  • The game is rated M (Mature 17+) by the ESRB and PEGI 18 in Europe.

GTA 6 PC release date: is it coming?

Not officially, at least not yet. Rockstar has not announced a PC version, and the console launch came and went without a word about one. That also means there are no official PC system requirements, PC pre-orders or PC store pages.

The most specific claim so far is a GamesBeat report, picked up by TechRadar in October, which says Rockstar plans a PC release in the first quarter of 2027 based on four anonymous industry sources. Rockstar hasn't confirmed it.

History suggests patience. GTA 5 reached PC about 19 months after its console debut and Red Dead Redemption 2 roughly a year after. PC Gamer doesn't expect a PC launch before November 2027, and GameSpot's own estimate is early 2028. Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick has said Rockstar serves console players first, while also noting that PC can account for 45 to 50 percent of a major launch's sales.

Tip

Want GTA 6 on a PC or laptop today? Use Remote Play from your own PS5 or Xbox. Xbox has said the game won't stream to PC through Xbox Cloud Gaming.

How much is GTA 6? Prices and editions

In the US, the Standard Edition costs $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition $99.99 on both the PlayStation Store and the Xbox Store. Here's what the extra $20 gets you, followed by every edition and add-on price:

  • Ultimate adds vehicles such as the '95 Grotti Cheetah and '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy, the Hawk and Little Morgan revolvers, engraved pistol variants, outfit packs and the Ganado Retro Build for Jason's pickup.
  • It also opens places and activities Standard owners can't use: the Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's mod shops, Sara's Unisex Salon, the Stock 305 clothing store, Electric Fang Tattoo, the PTT Youngin$ Compound raid and the Classic Car Collection side activity.
  • Ultimate items are spread across the story, so many only become available in later chapters.
  • Digital copies bought before November 20, 2026, included the Vintage Vice City Pack and a month of GTA+. That launch offer has ended, and the pack is now sold separately.

Is there a GTA 6 disc? The physical edition explained

No. The boxed version of GTA 6 contains a download code instead of a disc, and there's no physical Ultimate Edition. Boxed Standard owners can still buy the upgrade on the PlayStation or Microsoft store after redeeming their code.

Mind the region if you buy a boxed copy abroad. Rockstar says PlayStation codes only work on accounts registered in the matching region, while Xbox codes aren't region-locked.

GTA 6 file size, download and preload

Rockstar says you need up to 212 GB of free space, depending on your console. GamesRadar puts the PS5 install at about 210.5 GB, and updates will add to that over time. The first patch arrived on November 23.

Pre-loading opened on November 10 for digital pre-orders, at midnight local time on PS5 and at 12 a.m. GMT on Xbox Series X|S. If you're buying now, clear the full 212 GB before you start the download.

GTA 6 on PS5 Pro: 60fps and performance modes

GTA 6 runs at 30fps on every console, and there's no performance mode at launch. According to PC Gamer, Rockstar technology production director Marc Chambers called an official 60fps mode too much for current consoles, PS5 Pro included.

What changes between consoles is image quality and stability. Here's how Digital Foundry's tests break down, as reported by TechRadar and Kotaku:

Players found an Xbox glitch that lifts the 30fps cap, but Digital Foundry measured only about 36fps on average with big swings, so it's no substitute for a real performance mode.

Is GTA 6 online? Multiplayer status

Not yet. GTA 6 is a single-player game at launch, and both the PlayStation and Xbox store listings describe it that way. Rockstar hasn't announced a GTA 6 Online mode or a date for one, so treat any talk of a multiplayer launch as rumor until Rockstar says otherwise.

GTA 6 release & platforms FAQ

Is GTA 6 on PS4?

No. GTA 6 is a PS5 game, and the PlayStation Store listing states it won't be available on PS4.

Can you play GTA 6 on Xbox One?

Only through Xbox Cloud Gaming. The game doesn't run natively on Xbox One, so you need to own it and have cloud playtime, and the stream uses Series S settings.

Is GTA 6 on PC or Steam?

No. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version, so there's no Steam page or official PC requirements. A GamesBeat report points to early 2027, but Rockstar hasn't confirmed it.

Is GTA 6 on Game Pass?

No. Microsoft's FAQ says you have to buy GTA 6, and Game Pass members can only use their cloud hours to stream a copy they own.

Does GTA 6 run at 60fps?

No. It's capped at 30fps on PS5, PS5 Pro and both Xbox Series consoles, with no performance mode at launch.

How many GB is GTA 6?

Rockstar lists up to 212 GB of free space depending on your console, and GamesRadar puts the PS5 install at about 210.5 GB.

Can I upgrade GTA 6 Standard to Ultimate later?

Yes. The Ultimate Edition Upgrade is sold separately on the PlayStation and Microsoft stores and is listed at $20 on the Xbox Store.

Does GTA 6 come on a disc?

No. Physical copies contain a download code in the box, and there is no physical Ultimate Edition.
