GTA 6 Characters: Jason, Lucia and the Confirmed Cast

Meet the GTA 6 characters: Jason, Lucia and the Rockstar-confirmed supporting cast, who voices them per the official credits, and how switching works.

Updated Nov 24, 20266 min read9 sources checked
QUICK ANSWER

GTA 6 has two playable leads: Jason Duval, an Army veteran doing jobs for drug runners in the Keys, and Lucia Caminos, a trained fighter fresh out of prison. Rockstar's official credits name Dylan Rourke as Jason and Manni L. Perez as Lucia, and you can switch between the two freely from Chapter 2 onward, outside story-locked missions.

Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

GTA 6 follows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, two partners who end up in the middle of a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida after an easy score goes wrong. Rockstar's narrative chief described it to Dazed as a Bonnie and Clyde-style story, and Dazed notes it is the first time a GTA game has put a playable couple in the lead.

As in GTA 5, you can swap between protagonists, but here the pair can also roam together, split up, or trade control in the middle of a chase. Below is the spoiler-light cast Rockstar has officially introduced, plus who plays each role according to the credits Rockstar published on launch day.

Jason Duval: the guy who wants an easy life

Jason grew up around grifters and criminals, did a stint in the Army, and drifted back to the Keys, where he picks up work from local drug runners. Rockstar pitches him as someone who keeps chasing a quiet life while his problems keep piling up, and who is starting to wonder if it is time for a change.

His world starts small. He lives rent-free in a property owned by Brian Heder in exchange for helping with shakedowns, and his closest friend is Cal Hampton. Lucia could be his ticket out or his undoing. You play Chapter 1 almost entirely as Jason, so he is your first guide to Leonida.

Lucia Caminos: the fighter with a plan

Trained by her father to fight from early childhood, Lucia ended up behind bars at Leonida Penitentiary after standing up for her family. Out on a lucky break, she is determined to play it smart this time and to build the comfortable life her mother has dreamed about since their Liberty City days. A future with Jason looks like her way out.

Dazed calls Lucia the first fully fleshed-out female lead in a mainline GTA game. Her actor, Manni L. Perez, was first publicly tied to the role through a Best Performance nomination at The Game Awards, according to Kotaku.

GTA 6 supporting characters confirmed by Rockstar

Before launch, Rockstar introduced six more key characters on its official GTA 6 site. Here is the gist of each, without story spoilers.

  • Cal Hampton: Jason's paranoid, easygoing buddy, happiest at home eavesdropping on Coast Guard radio and fine with life at the bottom. He also works with Brian.
  • Brian Heder: a drug runner from the Keys' old smuggling days who still moves product through his boatyard with his third wife, Lori, while younger people take the risks. He is Jason's landlord.
  • Boobie Ike: a Vice City legend who turned street money into a business empire of real estate, a strip club and a recording studio. Warm and friendly until business comes up.
  • Dre'Quan Priest: a hustler at heart rather than a gangster, he chased a music career the whole time and now runs Only Raw Records with Boobie.
  • Real Dimez (Bae-Luxe and Roxy): high-school friends turned rap duo, known for provocative tracks, a big social media following and a breakout single with local rapper DWNPLY. Now signed to Only Raw, they need a follow-up hit.
  • Raul Bautista: a smooth, confident veteran bank robber who recruits people willing to take big risks for big money. His recklessness raises the stakes on every job.
Tip

Side characters from the credits such as Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone each have optional questlines with their own trophies. Our trophy guide covers which ones count.

How does character switching work in GTA 6?

Switching unlocks with the story. Chapter 1 is played as Jason apart from a few short scenes where you briefly control Lucia, and free switching in the open world opens up during Chapter 2. Guides disagree on the exact trigger: PowerPyx says it unlocks after the main mission Coffee Break, while KeenGamer points to Breakfast Run.

  1. Hold Right on the D-pad to open the character menu.
  2. Keep holding and press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox Series X|S to jump to the other lead.
  3. Want them together instead? Press Triangle on PS5 or Y on Xbox in the same menu, and your partner heads to your location.
  4. If the partner's portrait is greyed out, they are busy or unavailable, which often happens at home. Wait a moment or step outside, then try again.
  5. Check mission icons on the map before starting a job, because some missions have to be started as a specific character.
Tip

A quick tap of D-pad Right detonates placed explosives, so hold the button when you mean to switch.

Playing as a duo vs. going solo

Rob Nelson, co-studio head at Rockstar North, told Famitsu that the story decides when you can switch: some missions lock you to one character, some make you swap at key moments, and others let you change whenever it makes sense. In free roam, you choose whether Jason and Lucia travel as a pair or split up to cause trouble on their own.

Switching shines in vehicles. Dazed describes driving as Jason, then hopping over to Lucia in the passenger seat to use her phone, or flipping to the passenger mid-chase to shoot at pursuers. Each lead also keeps separate stats: the Personal Best trophy wants maximum Strength on both Jason and Lucia, so split your workouts, and Hot Together asks you to escape a four-star wanted level as a team.

GTA 6 characters FAQ

Who are the two main characters in GTA 6?

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Jason is an ex-Army man working for drug runners in the Keys, and Lucia is a trained fighter recently released from Leonida Penitentiary; the story follows them as partners.

Who voices Jason in GTA 6?

Dylan Rourke. Rockstar's official GTA 6 credits list him first in the main cast as Jason Duval, and he acknowledged the role on launch day.

Who plays Lucia in GTA 6?

Manni L. Perez is credited as Lucia Caminos in Rockstar's official credits. She is not the same person as Manny Perez, who plays Raul Bautista.

Who voices Cal in GTA 6?

Bobby Moynihan is credited as Cal, Jason's stay-at-home friend who listens in on Coast Guard radio.

Can you switch between Jason and Lucia at any time?

Not at first. Chapter 1 is played almost entirely as Jason, free switching opens up in Chapter 2, and some missions still lock you to one character.

What button switches characters in GTA 6?

Hold D-pad Right and press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox. In the same menu, Triangle or Y calls your partner to meet up with you.

Is Lucia the first female protagonist in GTA?

Dazed describes her as the first fully fleshed-out female lead in a mainline GTA game. GTA 6 is also the first entry built around a playable couple, according to the same feature.
