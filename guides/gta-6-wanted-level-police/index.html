GTA 6 wanted level and Heat explained: how police track you and how to lose them

GTA 6 wanted level guide: what the stars and red Heat icons mean, what sets police on you, how to lose the cops fast and what getting Busted costs you.

Updated Nov 24, 20267 min read22 sources checked
QUICK ANSWER

GTA 6 police only respond once a crime is witnessed and reported or trips an alarm, and red Heat icons under your stars show what they know: your face, clothes, weapon, vehicle, CCTV footage or your partner. To lose them, break line of sight, leave the red search area and clear every Heat icon while nobody is watching.

How the wanted level works in GTA 6

The wanted meter now goes up to six stars, and it runs on information rather than instant omniscience. Police don't automatically know where you are when you commit a crime. Someone has to see it, report it or set off an alarm, and then a blue search area appears on the map. Once officers spot you, that area turns red and widens.

Two more changes matter. Cops don't show up on the minimap anymore; Rockstar North's Rob Nelson explained before launch that you find them with Focus instead. And police memory lasts: details they've collected can linger after your stars fade, so a cleared wanted level doesn't always mean you're in the clear.

Star look (as reported)What it means
Hollow starsA crime was reported, but police don't know what you look like
Solid white starsOfficers can see you right now
Flashing white starsYou broke line of sight, but the chase is still on
Red starsPolice don't know where you are, but they're still searching nearby
Tip

Outlets describe the in-between star styles a little differently, so treat the Heat icons under your stars as your real checklist.

What triggers a wanted level in GTA 6?

Police act on information, so anything that creates a report or a description of you can start a wanted level:

  • Witnesses: crimes have to be seen, and bystanders will call in your location.
  • Alarms: smashing the window of an alarmed car or forcing an alarmed door draws attention. Focus shows alarmed doors in red.
  • Security cameras: CCTV records robberies and hands police footage of you.
  • Gunfire: reported shots bring officers, and killings speed up the response.
  • Car trackers: stolen cars with an advanced (red) tracker ping your location to the police if you slow down.
  • Trespassing and big stunts: restricted areas count, and stealing a passenger jet from the airport or an APC from a military base pushes you to the top of the meter.
  • Visible weapons: walking around armed changes how people react and can end up in the description police receive.

GTA 6 Heat icons explained

Heat icons sit under your stars and show exactly which details the police are using to find you. The more you have, the harder it is to slip away. Each one has its own fix:

FaceWhat you look likePut on a mask or bandana before the crime and take it off afterwards; a new hairstyle also works
ClothingYour outfitChange from your car's trunk, a clothing store fitting room or an Ammu-Nation locker
WeaponYou were seen armedHolster your pistol and stow or drop long guns; owned guns you drop return to your trunk
VehicleYour carDitch it, swap cars without being seen or pay for a respray at Pay 'n' Spray

Tip

Heat is also tracked by district. Hover over red-tinted zones on the map to see which icons are still active there; they fade after a while.

How to lose the cops in GTA 6

Losing the police is about erasing what they know, not just outrunning them. Work through these steps:

  1. Break line of sight. Cut through alleys, hop fences, run through backyards or weave through traffic.
  2. Get out of the red search area. The compass bar shows the red zone too, so head toward the part that isn't red.
  3. Clear your Heat icons while nobody is watching. GameSpot found that once every factor is dealt with out of sight, the police can't identify you.
  4. Stay in an enclosed car. Walking or riding a motorbike near officers makes you easier to recognize.
  5. Lie low. In a car, hold Circle/B to duck down while police search the area.
  6. Respray or swap your ride. Pay 'n' Spray won't let you in while officers can see you, so lose them first.
  7. Split from your partner if the Accomplice icon is showing. If either character gets caught while you're apart, you're still Busted.
  8. Wait it out. Stay outside the search area and the remaining heat fades.
Tip

Wanted level that never clears? Rockstar's November 23 update fixed a bug that left players unable to lose their Wanted Level, so make sure your game is updated.

Low wanted levels: talk your way out or surrender

At one star, you may not need to run at all. With no gun drawn, hold L2/LT on an officer to get defuse or provoke options. Kotaku found that defusing can wipe a one-star wanted level, although some officers will still attack.

At low levels, police mostly use tasers, stun guns and batons, and they can drag you out of a slow-moving car. You can also surrender: holster your weapon, walk up to the police and press R1/RB, or mash the on-screen prompt if you'd rather resist arrest.

Your criminal profile matters here. Kill lots of cops and civilians and you can slide into the Psycho or Irredeemable profiles, which take away the option to surrender and make police open fire sooner. Irredeemable is permanent.

Five and six stars: when the military joins in

The top of the meter is reserved for serious chaos. PC Gamer notes that hijacking a passenger jet at the airport or an APC at a military base gets you to the highest levels, and five stars brings out a tank.

Six stars only arrives after mass destruction and heavy loss of life. Once you have it, there's no escape: police and the military keep coming until you're taken down, and GamesRadar's six-star run ended with jets shooting down a hijacked plane. Even the wanted-level cheat tops out at five stars.

Tip

Going for six stars on purpose? Save first. The only way out is getting Wasted, which costs you money and any gear you don't own.

What happens when you get Busted or Wasted?

Getting arrested or killed doesn't end your game, but it isn't free. After an arrest you respawn at the last safehouse you visited. These are the penalties guides have reported:

  • Money: you lose cash. GamesRadar says all of your pocket cash goes, GameSpot says some cash plus money from your BuckMe account, and PC Gamer describes a penalty of a few hundred dollars. Either way, bank big sums.
  • Gear: weapons and clothes you only picked up are gone for good. Guns you bought and items stored in your car stay safe.
  • Loot: stash bags you're carrying when you die are lost.
  • Your car: the vehicle you were using can end up impounded. Scooter Bros can retrieve it for a significant fee, or you can try to steal it back from the lot.

How to avoid heat in the first place

The easiest wanted level to lose is the one you never get. A little prep before any crime goes a long way:

  • Buy a mask early. Chuck's Tackle & Shot in Key Lento sells them from the start, as do Ammu-Nation stores; set the wear style so it covers your face.
  • Keep a spare outfit and your long guns in your car's trunk, so you can change and stow weapons quickly.
  • Fit a suppressor. Reported gunshots bring the police, so quiet weapons buy you time.
  • Check a store's security level on the map before robbing it, and look for a back door.
  • Skip cars with a red tracker icon until you can deal with trackers.
  • Listen to the police scanner. The chatter tells you when officers have reported your car or your clothes.

GTA 6 wanted level FAQ

What is the max wanted level in GTA 6?

Six stars. Five stars already brings out a tank, and six stars only comes after mass destruction and heavy loss of life.

How do you lose a 6-star wanted level in GTA 6?

You can't. At six stars the police and military never stop chasing you, so the only way out is getting Wasted. Save before you try it.

Why can't I lose my wanted level in GTA 6?

Usually a Heat icon is still active or you're still inside the red search area. Fix what the icons show while out of sight, and install the November 23 update, which fixed a bug that blocked losing the Wanted Level.

Do cops show up on the minimap in GTA 6?

No. The map shows the search area instead of individual officers, and you use Focus to spot police around you.

Can you surrender to the police in GTA 6?

Yes, unless your criminal profile is Psycho or Irredeemable. Holster your weapon, approach an officer and press R1/RB, then wait to be cuffed.

What do you lose when you get Busted in GTA 6?

You respawn at your last safehouse having lost some or all of your pocket cash, plus any weapons and clothes you didn't own. Your car may also be impounded.

Does a Pay 'n' Spray remove your wanted level in GTA 6?

A respray clears your vehicle heat so police stop recognizing the car, but you can't drive in while officers can still see you.
