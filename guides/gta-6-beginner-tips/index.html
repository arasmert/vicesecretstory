GTA 6 beginner tips: the essentials for your first hours in Leonida

GTA 6 beginner tips that save time and cash: how saving works, how to heal, the phone apps you need, settings worth changing and early mistakes to avoid.

Updated Nov 24, 20267 min read27 sources checked
QUICK ANSWER

Make manual saves from the pause menu (Game, then Save Game), because GTA 6 keeps only one autosave and never saves mid-mission. Bank your cash once BuckMe unlocks, buy your guns at Ammu-Nation instead of relying on pickups, use Focus constantly, and clear Key Lento's side content before Chapter 1 wraps up.

Things to know before playing GTA 6

GTA 6 breaks plenty of GTA 5 habits. These are the changes that catch new players out most often:

  • The default controls are new: sprint is on L3/LS, X/A jumps and Square/X is melee. A Classic scheme closer to GTA 5 is in the settings.
  • The HUD hides itself. Tap Down on the D-pad to bring up the minimap, your money and the health, stamina and Focus bars.
  • Police don't show up on the minimap. Rockstar North's Rob Nelson explained before launch that you spot them with Focus instead.
  • There's no instant map-warp fast travel. Taxis, buses, trains and later the RydeMe app get you around.
  • Missions can't be replayed, so your save slots are the only way back to earlier moments.
  • You can't freely switch between Jason and Lucia until Chapter 2.
  • Cheats only unlock after you beat the main story, and they disable saving plus trophies and achievements.
  • It's single-player at launch, with no GTA 6 Online mode yet.

Best GTA 6 settings to change first

Before you leave Key Lento, spend two minutes on these options:

  • Control scheme: switch from Default to Classic if GTA 5 muscle memory keeps making you jump when you meant to sprint.
  • HUD: the Dynamic HUD hides the minimap and stat bars until you tap Down on the D-pad. You can toggle those elements on in the settings.
  • Navigation: if you play without the minimap, turn on the compass and choose a GPS voice, a tone or both for turn-by-turn directions.
  • Motion blur (Settings, Display): some players set it to 0 for a sharper image, while others on OLED TVs keep it around 4 to 6 to smooth the 30fps motion.
  • Gameplay tab: there's no difficulty selector, but Accessible Combat makes you tougher and enemies weaker, and Reduced Vehicle Damage keeps your cars running after crashes. The game warns that both significantly change the experience.
Tip

Neither assist is a one-way door. GameSpot notes you can always switch back to the default gameplay options if things start to feel too easy.

How to save in GTA 6

GTA 6 has 15 manual save slots plus a single autosave slot that keeps overwriting itself. The autosave triggers after story missions and bigger side missions, and also after you're Wasted or Busted, but Kotaku found that small errands don't trigger it and nothing is saved partway through a mission.

You can't save during missions, in combat or while the police are chasing you. Loading a manual save may also drop you near the spot where you saved rather than exactly on it. Rockstar's November 23 update fixed a bug that stopped the game from autosaving correctly, but saving by hand is still the safest habit.

There's no quit option in the menus, so save before you close the game from your console's home screen.

  1. Pause the game with the Options or Menu button.
  2. Select the Game option.
  3. Pick one of the 15 save slots.
  4. Choose Save Game.
Tip

Keep one save at the start of every chapter. Missions can't be replayed, and each chapter is built around a different safehouse.

How to heal in GTA 6 and keep your stats up

Jason and Lucia run on three core stats: Health, Stamina and Focus. Focus is the meter that lets you slow time while you aim.

In a fight, hold L1/LB to open your loadout, then press Square/X to heal or Triangle/Y to use Adrenaline, which briefly boosts health, stamina and Focus. Healing products such as Zombix get weaker if you keep popping them, so duck into cover instead of spamming them. Zombix can be found for free in the world, and Kotaku notes that stores usually keep it behind the counter, so ask the clerk.

Avoid damage for a short while and health refills slowly on its own, but only up to the solid white line on the bar, and more slowly when your stamina is low.

Tip

Build a routine at the start of each session: sleep, eat, shower and work out. GameSpot says those buffs last until you quit or die.

GTA 6 phone basics

Tap Up on the D-pad to pull out your phone. The Reminders feed (Up, then Square/X) gathers texts, social posts and calls worth answering. These are the apps you'll use most early on:

AppWhat it's for
BuckMeYour bank. It unlocks during the Chapter 1 mission Buck Me, includes an ATM Finder, and covers any purchase over $1,000.
WAiNKHold Up on the D-pad to scan a car for locks, alarms, keyless entry, trackers, resale value and registration cost. Cal installs it during the mission Unwanted Attention.
Scooter BrosDelivers, recovers and repairs your registered vehicles and can get them out of the impound lot, all for a fee. Premium costs $12,500 and adds three free uses per in-game day.
RydeMeRide-hailing from Chapter 2. Pay $100 extra to skip the trip and arrive instantly.
FitnessTracks your workouts and tells you when today's gains are maxed out.
Police scannerA homebrew app Cal installs early, so you can hear the police talking about you.
Tip

Gas stations fix cars too: pull up to a pump, open Services and pick Repair. Kotaku found it's often cheaper than calling Scooter Bros.

Money tips for your first chapters

Cash is tight early on, so protect what you earn:

  • Each character can carry up to $20,000 in pocket cash, and cash only covers purchases up to $1,000.
  • Before you have a bank account, stash money under the floorboards in the bedroom of Jason's house in Key Lento.
  • Getting Wasted or Busted costs you money. Guides disagree on how much, from a few hundred dollars to everything in your pocket, so deposit big sums at an ATM.
  • Still keep a few hundred dollars on you. Some street encounters and bribes need cash in hand, and a few vendors take nothing else.
  • Money paid into BuckMe Family Savings can never be taken out again, so only add what you won't need.
  • Sell loot to a fence soon after every big job. If you die while carrying stash bags, they're gone.
  • Don't grind for money in the opening hours; spend it on gear you'll use right away, like a suppressor.

Common beginner mistakes to avoid

Most early frustrations in GTA 6 come from a handful of avoidable slip-ups:

  1. Treating picked-up guns as your arsenal. Weapons you take from enemies are lost when you're Wasted or Busted, while guns you buy at Ammu-Nation can always be recovered from your trunk or a weapons locker.
  2. Stealing cars with a red tracker icon. Advanced trackers ping the police whenever you slow down, so leave those cars alone early on.
  3. Robbing banks or loan stores before Chapter 3. Most of their cash is rigged with dye packs, and the jammer that beats them only arrives in Chapter 3.
  4. Going in loud. Reported gunshots bring the police, so a suppressor buys you time to loot.
  5. Kicking in doors blind. Focus (click both sticks) shows enemies in red, usable items in yellow and cash in green, and alarmed doors glow red too.
  6. Leaving Key Lento's side content for later. GamesRadar warns that some Chapter 1 activities and collectibles lock once the mission Tourist Trap ends the chapter, and the drive back to the Keys can take more than 10 minutes.
  7. Sleeping through timed missions. Check the Index for activities marked with an hourglass before you sleep to skip time.
Tip

Leonida is a version of Florida, so take Kotaku's advice and assume there's an alligator in every body of water.

GTA 6 beginner tips FAQ

How do you save in GTA 6?

Pause the game, open Game and choose Save Game in one of the 15 manual slots. You can't save during missions, in combat or while the police are chasing you.

Does GTA 6 have difficulty settings?

No, there are no easy or hard modes. The Gameplay settings do include Accessible Combat and Reduced Vehicle Damage, which make fights and driving more forgiving.

How do you heal in GTA 6?

Hold L1/LB to open your loadout, then press Square/X to heal or Triangle/Y for Adrenaline. Food marked with a pink heart also restores health, and your bar slowly regenerates up to its white line.

Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

No. GTA 6 has no mission replay option, so keep a manual save at the start of each chapter if you want to revisit earlier moments.

When do cheats unlock in GTA 6?

Only after you finish the main story, and each cheat then has its own challenge to complete. Using cheats disables saving and trophies or achievements until you reload.

Does GTA 6 have fast travel?

Not the instant map-warp kind. You can ride buses and trains, pay $100 extra to skip a taxi or RydeMe trip, or, from Chapter 2, switch to the other protagonist, who usually waits near your safehouse.

Can you play GTA 6 in first person?

Not officially at launch. You can hold L2/LT and press R3 to look around in first person while standing still, and PC Gamer reports a glitch that unlocks a rough full first-person mode.
