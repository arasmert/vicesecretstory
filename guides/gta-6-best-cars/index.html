GTA 6 Best Cars: The Fastest Rides and Where to Find Them

GTA 6 best cars ranked by GameSpot's in-game speed ratings, plus two free Progen Emerus locations, rare rides, garage prices and how to keep stolen cars.

Updated Nov 24, 20267 min read28 sources checked
QUICK ANSWER

Based on GameSpot's in-game testing, the Truffade Thrax is the fastest stock car in GTA 6 with a speed rating of 8.7 out of 10, ahead of the Progen Emerus at 8.3. The Emerus is the best fast car you can realistically own: two can be stolen with no alarm or tracker, and a fully upgraded one reaches 9.0. You can start registering stolen cars as your own after meeting Moses in Chapter 2.

What is the fastest car in GTA 6?

GameSpot ranks cars by their in-game speed rating, a score out of 10, using stock cars with no upgrades. Performance parts can push a rating to 9.0 or higher. We haven't seen tested mph figures from a major outlet, so treat quoted top speeds with caution.

GameSpot hasn't confirmed the Thrax can be upgraded, so a fully tuned Emerus at 9.0 is the quickest car most players can build and keep.

Best cars in GTA 6 by class and role

No outlet has ranked every class yet; GameSpot's list covers cars only. These picks come from hands-on guides:

  • Supercar: the Truffade Thrax for stock speed, or the Progen Emerus for the best upgraded speed you can realistically own (GameSpot).
  • Sports car: the Pfister Comet S2, the only non-supercar in GameSpot's top five.
  • Early street car: Paul Walker's Fast and Furious Mitsubishi, which GamesRadar says handles really well, hits high speeds and is free to steal.
  • Free first registration: the Invetero Coquette '64 at Fontana Motorsports in Capri, with no alarm or tracker and a normal registration fee of $29,150 (GameSpot).
  • Street-race starter: the GTA+ '90s Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy, a tuned widebody that comes with a Crosstown garage (GamesRadar).

How to get the Progen Emerus for free

GameSpot found two special Emerus cars that are unlocked, with no alarm or tracker, and available almost from the start. You just can't keep a stolen car until registration opens after you meet Moses in Chapter 2.

  1. Purple Emerus: on Waxman Island, east of downtown Vice City, go to the Robbery Opportunity at Chatfield Storage & Logistics on the west side. The car pulls into a guarded yard; clear it and drive off. No robbery icon yet? Progress the story and come back.
  2. Royal Ruby Emerus: on Vespucci Island in the Rialto Islands, find Neptune Circle on the west side, just south of the main road. Hop the gate of the large house; the car is in the garage on its south side.
  3. Register it with Moses or at a Pay 'n' Spray ($29K and $29,900 in GameSpot's game), then upgrade it at a higher-end Mod Shop.
Tip

Each Emerus sells for over $30,000, so keeping one and selling the other covers the registration fee.

Where to get the Truffade Thrax and other hidden rides

Reports vary on the Thrax. Game Rant traced one to a private Starfish Island garage at the end of a hidden quest that starts by scanning 10 No Flusher tags around Vice Beach, with a $70,000 registration fee. GameSpot's guides don't cover how to get one, so treat this as a single-source tip.

Collectors can also earn a unique 1010 Cheetah and the Southside Storage garage by finding all five 1010 murals (PowerPyx). GameSpot reports Tommy Vercetti's classic bike rewards a long mystery that starts at the radio tower west of Key Lento. After the story, challenge-unlocked cheats can spawn the Emerus and Thrax (GamesRadar).

Ultimate Edition and pre-order vehicles

Bonus vehicles unlock through the story rather than on day one, per GamesRadar and PC Gamer:

  • Ultimate Edition: a Dinka Enduro motorcycle and Crest Kayak at Jason's house (Chapter 1); the Ganado Retro Build at Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento (Chapter 1 only); the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy and Paradise Garage after a Mud Club race with Wyman, which opens his Classic Car Collection (Chapter 2); the Shitzu Squalo boat (Chapter 4); and the '95 Grotti Cheetah under the Pegassi Residences (Chapter 5).
  • Vintage Vice City Pack (pre-orders and digital purchases made before November 20, 2026): a '55 Vapid Stanier and garage, via an Exploration task from Chapter 3.

How to save cars in GTA 6: registering and storing

A stolen car isn't yours until it's registered, and GameSpot warns that unregistered cars eventually vanish, especially after you quit or a mission moves on.

  1. Reach Chapter 2. After the Girls' Trip mission you meet Moses, a Port Gellhorn vehicle fence who gives you the Slim Jim and registers your first car for free.
  2. Scan first. The WAiNK app shows a car's value, condition, security and trackers.
  3. Skip tracked cars early, since Moses won't take them at first. Yellow standard trackers let police trace you when you stop, red advanced ones whenever you drive too slowly, and red-tracker cars can't be owned until you're far into Randy's Wish List.
  4. Register with Moses, or later at a Pay 'n' Spray. Fees scale with the car and can reach tens of thousands of dollars.
  5. Registered cars show up in the Personal tab of the map Index, and the Scooter Bros app delivers them, or recovers destroyed or impounded ones, for a fee.
Tip

Can't pay yet? Park outside your safehouse or in a garage to keep the car around (GamesRadar), though red-tracker cars can't be garaged until registered (GameSpot).

GTA 6 garage locations and prices

Each garage slot lets you register another car, and garages with a weapon locker let you swap your loadout and drop off stolen goods. The Vice City Real Estate app arrives soon after the Chapter 2 mission Daily Driver. PowerPyx counts 13 garages, eight of them for sale at $160,000 combined, for up to 18 vehicle slots.

How car customization works in GTA 6

PowerPyx counts 14 Mod Shops, each specializing in a vehicle type such as tuners, lowriders or dirt bikes. In the map Index, a blue wrench beside a shop's name means it specializes in your current ride and stocks exclusive parts (GamesRadar).

Engine, transmission and other performance parts raise the speed rating, while the Cosmetics tab handles paint, liveries, decals and plates. Cars with a standard tracker can't be modified until you register them.

A Pay 'n' Spray respray clears your Vehicle Heat, though you can't drive in while police can see you. Ultimate Edition owners also get Rideout Customs in Crosstown for donk-style rims and interiors, and One-Eyed Willie's in North Leonard County for off-road builds.

Tip

Fuel is new: press Down on the D-pad to check fuel and engine damage. Gas stations refuel and partly repair cars; electric cars need EV chargers (GamesRadar).

GTA 6 best cars FAQ

What is the fastest car in GTA 6?

In GameSpot's testing, the Truffade Thrax has the top stock speed rating at 8.7 out of 10. A fully upgraded Progen Emerus reaches 9.0, the best most players can actually own.

Can you get a free supercar in GTA 6?

Yes. GameSpot found two Progen Emerus cars with no alarm or tracker, on Waxman Island and Vespucci Island. Stealing them is free, but registering one costs about $29,000.

How do you save a car in GTA 6?

Register it with Moses after meeting him in Chapter 2, or later at a Pay 'n' Spray. Until then, park it outside your safehouse or in a garage so it doesn't disappear.

How many cars can you own in GTA 6?

PowerPyx counts up to 18 vehicle slots once you own every garage: 16 inside garages plus two personal vehicles parked outside.

What is the best car to register first in GTA 6?

GameSpot suggests the Invetero Coquette '64 behind Fontana Motorsports in Capri. It has no alarm or tracker and normally costs $29,150 to register, the best use of your free registration.

Where is Paul Walker's car in GTA 6?

It's in a locked garage on Vespucci Island, west of Vice Beach. GamesRadar suggests it as your free first registration, but GameSpot warns the registration respray removes its unique livery.

How many cars are in GTA 6?

Reports vary. GamesRadar counts over 157 cars and 29 types of bikes, while PowerPyx says the WAiNK tracker lists 277 base models plus 280 standout vehicles.
