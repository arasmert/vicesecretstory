Is GTA 6 Online Out? What Rockstar Has Actually Confirmed

Is GTA 6 Online out? No. See what Rockstar has confirmed about GTA 6 multiplayer, what the reported 2027 window rests on, and what happens to GTA Online.

Updated Nov 24, 20265 min read13 sources checked
QUICK ANSWER

No. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026 as a single-player game, and Rockstar has not announced an online mode, a name or a release date for one. Bloomberg reported in October that the online component is expected in 2027, but Rockstar hasn't confirmed that, and the original GTA Online on GTA 5 keeps getting updates in the meantime.

Is GTA 6 Online out?

Not yet. Grand Theft Auto VI arrived on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026 with no multiplayer of any kind. The PlayStation and Xbox store pages carry the same line from Rockstar: "Grand Theft Auto VI is a single-player experience." The PlayStation Store lists it as a one-player game with offline play supported.

For context, GTA Online arrived about two weeks after GTA 5 back in 2013. Rockstar hasn't signaled any similar plan for GTA 6, and neither the official GTA 6 site nor the launch announcement mentions an online mode.

What Rockstar has actually confirmed

  • GTA 6 is single-player only, per the FAQs on the PlayStation Store and Xbox listings.
  • No online mode, name, price or date has been announced.
  • It is out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, with Xbox One covered by cloud streaming.
  • No PC version has been announced. In May 2026, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said Rockstar had announced GTA 6 for consoles only so far.
  • It is not part of Xbox Game Pass; Xbox's FAQ says you have to buy it.
Tip

Official GTA 6 news shows up on Rockstar's Newswire and the official GTA 6 site. If a release date isn't there, treat it as a guess.

Online features GTA 6 does have

There is no multiplayer, but a few connected features already work.

  • Cloud streaming on PlayStation: the PS5 listing says streaming on PS5 and PlayStation Portal requires a PlayStation Plus Premium subscription.
  • Xbox Cloud Gaming: if you own the game, you can stream it on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S with cloud playtime. Game Pass members get monthly cloud time, and in supported markets anyone can buy it. Streams use Xbox Series S graphics settings.
  • Remote Play is supported, per the PlayStation Store listing.
  • GTA+, Rockstar's console subscription, now adds optional single-player perks for Jason and Lucia, per the official GTA 6 site.
  • Some features may need an internet connection and a Rockstar Games account, according to Xbox Wire, though the PS5 version supports offline play.

GTA 6 Online release date: what the reports say

Every claim about timing so far comes from third parties, not from Rockstar or Take-Two. Here is how the main ones stack up.

Twitch CEO Dan Clancy said he had talked with Rockstar about its plans and expects an even bigger viewing spike when multiplayer launches the year after GTA 6. Bloomberg's October feature on the game's development also said the online component is expected in 2027, but as Digital Trends pointed out, expected is not confirmed, and neither company has publicly backed that date.

Will GTA Online keep going after GTA 6?

Yes, for now. Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick said on the company's August 2026 earnings call that it expects to keep supporting GTA Online, with an update cadence that hasn't really changed. Back in May, he said the game would stay on the market after GTA 6 launched.

The updates haven't stopped. GTA Online got the Kortz Center Heist summer update in July 2026, according to Take-Two's quarterly results, and Rockstar's current event bonuses run through December 2.

What nobody has said is whether GTA Online characters, money or vehicles will ever carry over to a GTA 6 multiplayer mode. Nothing official exists on that, so be skeptical of anyone promising a transfer.

What's still unconfirmed about GTA 6 multiplayer

Until Rockstar speaks, all of these remain open questions. Datamines and leaks can be fun to follow, but they are not announcements.

  • Whether GTA 6 Online will be free with the game or sold separately.
  • Its name and release date.
  • Whether it will support crossplay or cross-progression.
  • Whether any GTA Online progress transfers.
  • Whether a PC version, and PC online play, is coming.

What to play while you wait

The single-player game already leans into partner play. You can roam Leonida as Jason and Lucia together, swap seats in the middle of a chase, and pull off jobs as a duo; the Hot Together trophy even asks you to escape a four-star wanted level with your partner. If you want to play with other people right now, GTA Online on GTA 5 is still live and still getting events.

Tip

Since trophies are single-player only, nothing on the GTA 6 trophy list is waiting on an online mode. You can chase the Platinum today.

GTA 6 Online FAQ

Is GTA 6 Online out?

No. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026 as a single-player game, and Rockstar has not released or announced an online mode.

When does GTA 6 Online come out?

There is no official date. Bloomberg reported it is expected in 2027 and Twitch's CEO predicted the same, but Rockstar and Take-Two haven't confirmed it.

Does GTA 6 have multiplayer or co-op?

No. The store listings describe it as a one-player, single-player experience, so the only teamwork is switching between Jason and Lucia and working with your computer-controlled partner.

Do I need PlayStation Plus or Game Pass to play GTA 6?

Not to play it. The PS5 version supports offline play, and GTA 6 isn't in Game Pass, so on Xbox you buy it outright. PS Plus Premium only matters if you want to cloud-stream it on PS5 or PlayStation Portal.

Is GTA Online shutting down because of GTA 6?

No shutdown has been announced. Take-Two says it expects to keep supporting GTA Online, and Rockstar is still running events with bonuses and discounts.

Can I transfer my GTA Online character to GTA 6?

Rockstar hasn't announced any GTA 6 online mode, so there is no transfer system to speak of yet.

Is GTA 6 on PC?

Not yet. GTA 6 is out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Take-Two said in May 2026 that Rockstar had only announced console versions so far.
