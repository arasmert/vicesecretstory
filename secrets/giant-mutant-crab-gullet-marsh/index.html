GTA 6 Secret #5: The Giant Mutant Crab You Can't Kill

There's a reason the antenna outpost in Gullet Marsh feels abandoned. Restore the power, log into the scientist's computer and a giant mutant crab rises out of the water — one you can't kill, only survive.

🥚 Easter Eggs🔫 Weapons💰 MoneyUpdated Nov 23, 20261 min read

Where to find it

📍

The research outpost with the tall antenna, Gullet Marsh.

Step by step

  1. 01

    Find the research outpost with the tall antenna in Gullet Marsh.

  2. 02

    Turn the power on outside.

  3. 03

    Read the scientist's note: the computer password is CLEAVES.

  4. 04

    Log into the computer — something huge comes out of the water.

  5. 05

    Survive the encounter, then open the safe with code 0072 for a unique SMG and marked cash.

Tips

  • Don't waste ammo trying to kill the crab. The goal is to survive.
  • Keep the safe code handy before you start: 0072.

FAQ

What is the computer password at the Gullet Marsh outpost?

CLEAVES. It's written on the scientist's note.

What is the safe code and what's inside?

0072. The safe holds a unique SMG and marked cash.
