GTA 6 Secret #6: Steal a Rhino Tank and Unlock a Hidden Trophy

The base that gave you a free fighter jet has one more trick. In the south hangars of Mile Zero Air Station the Rhino tanks are unlocked, and driving one for about 25 seconds unlocks a hidden trophy.

▶

Watch on YouTube · Instagram

Where to find it

📍 Mile Zero Air Station, Goose Key — the same base as the jet.

Step by step

  1. Go back to Mile Zero Air Station on Goose Key.
  2. Run to the south hangars: the Rhino tanks are lined up there and unlocked.
  3. Climb in through the front hatch.
  4. Drive for about 25 seconds and the trophy "Challenging Tank" unlocks.

Tips

  • You can't keep the tank — expect five stars fast.
  • About 25 seconds of driving is all the trophy needs, so focus on staying alive.

FAQ

How do you unlock the Challenging Tank trophy in GTA 6?

Take a Rhino tank from the south hangars at Mile Zero Air Station and drive it for about 25 seconds.

Can you keep the Rhino tank?

No. You get a five-star wanted level quickly.
