GTA 6 Secret #1: How to Get the Turnt Nipper .38 From a Tree

Most players drive straight past this one. High above the road at Bobcat Bight, a paramotor has crashed into a tree, and the wreck holds the Turnt Nipper .38 — a pistol you can grab long before the story hands you better weapons.

🔫 WeaponsUpdated Nov 23, 20261 min read
QUICK ANSWER

Go to Bobcat Bight on the west coast of Mariana County, look up for the paramotor stuck in a tree, shoot its lines and loot the wreck for the Turnt Nipper .38.

Where to find it

📍

Bobcat Bight, Mariana County — on the west coast, just south of Capri.

Open map →

Before you go

  • Any gun that can hit the lines
  • A ride to the west coast, south of Capri

Step by step

  1. 01

    Head to Bobcat Bight on the west coast of Mariana County, just south of the town of Capri.

  2. 02

    Look up into the trees: a crashed paramotor is tangled in the branches.

  3. 03

    Shoot the lines holding it to bring the wreck down.

  4. 04

    Loot the wreck to pick up the Turnt Nipper .38.

Tips

  • The wreck sits above eye level, so slow down and look up — it is easy to miss from a moving car.
  • You don't need to climb anything: shooting the lines is what brings it down.
  • Grab it early. An extra sidearm makes the opening hours of the story easier.

Common mistakes

  • Driving past at speed — the wreck sits above eye level
  • Searching the ground: it only drops after you shoot the lines

FAQ

Where is the Turnt Nipper .38 in GTA 6?

In a crashed paramotor stuck in a tree at Bobcat Bight, Mariana County, on the west coast just south of Capri.

How do you get the paramotor down?

Shoot the lines that hold it in the tree. It drops to the ground so you can loot it.
