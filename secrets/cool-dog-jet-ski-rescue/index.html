GTA 6 Secret #3: Get Lost at Sea and Cool Dog Saves You on a Jet Ski

Getting stranded at sea in GTA 6 isn't always the end. Swim far enough from the coast near Gambit Bay and you may hear barking: that's Cool Dog, coming to pick you up on a jet ski.

▶

Watch on YouTube · Instagram

Where to find it

📍 Gambit Bay, the Keys — the rescue works best here.

Step by step

  1. Go out to Gambit Bay in the Keys — the event works best there.
  2. Swim far from shore and leave your boat or vehicle behind.
  3. Stay in the water and wait until you hear barking.
  4. Cool Dog arrives on a jet ski, wearing sunglasses and a life vest. Climb on and don't jump off early.
  5. On his island he digs up a wallet with about $1,200.

Tips

  • Leave your ride completely — the rescue is for players stranded in the water.
  • Be patient and listen for the barking before you give up.
  • Stay on the jet ski for the whole ride, or you miss the island and the wallet.
