GTA 6 Secret #4: Paul Walker's Green Eclipse Is Hiding in a Garage

Fast & Furious fans, this one is for you. Tucked away in a garage at the Vespucci Island villas is a green Eclipse that matches Paul Walker's car from the first movie — and the trunk hides a nasty surprise.

Where to find it

📍 The villas on Vespucci Island.

Step by step

  1. Head to the villas on Vespucci Island.
  2. Break into the garage to find the green Eclipse.
  3. Smash the window and hotwire it.
  4. Before you drive away, open the trunk. There's a snake inside — literally.

Tips

  • Check the trunk first: the snake is part of the secret.
  • Pair it with Secret #3 for an afternoon of easter eggs across the Keys.
