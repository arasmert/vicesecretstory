All GTA 6 secrets

Every secret we've found so far, in order. Each guide has the location, the steps and a short video.

  • #1 · How to Get the Turnt Nipper .38 From a TreeA crashed paramotor stuck in a tree at Bobcat Bight hides the Turnt Nipper .38. Here's exactly where to look and how to get it early.
  • #2 · Steal a Fighter Jet Before Your Second MissionThe jets on the flight line at Mile Zero Air Station aren't locked. Here's how to get in and take off before the base reacts.
  • #3 · Get Lost at Sea and Cool Dog Saves You on a Jet SkiStranded in the water near Gambit Bay? Wait for the barking. Cool Dog arrives on a jet ski and leads you to a hidden wallet.
  • #4 · Paul Walker's Green Eclipse Is Hiding in a GarageA garage at the Vespucci Island villas hides a green Eclipse straight out of Fast & Furious — with a surprise in the trunk.
  • #5 · The Giant Mutant Crab You Can't KillPower up the antenna outpost in Gullet Marsh, use the password CLEAVES and survive the giant crab. The safe code is 0072.
  • #6 · Steal a Rhino Tank and Unlock a Hidden TrophyThe Rhino tanks in the south hangars of Mile Zero Air Station are unlocked. Drive one for about 25 seconds to pop the Challenging Tank trophy.
