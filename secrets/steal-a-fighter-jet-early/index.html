GTA 6 Secret #2: Steal a Fighter Jet Before Your Second Mission

You don't have to wait for the late game to fly a military jet. The jets on the flight line at Mile Zero Air Station are unlocked from the start, so you can take one before your second story mission — as long as you get off the ground quickly.

▶

Watch on YouTube · Instagram

Where to find it

📍 Mile Zero Air Station — Goose Key, Mariana County.

Step by step

  1. Drive to Mile Zero Air Station on Goose Key, Mariana County.
  2. Smash through the main gate, or jump the fence using the ramp by the southeast entrance.
  3. Head to the flight line. The jets parked there aren't locked, so you can climb straight in.
  4. Take off fast: you get an instant wanted level, and jets will chase you.

Tips

  • Use the ramp by the southeast entrance if you would rather skip the gate.
  • Don't wait on the runway. The wanted level starts as soon as you take off and other jets follow.
  • The same base hides another secret — see Secret #6, the Rhino tank.

FAQ

Where can you steal a jet in GTA 6?

At Mile Zero Air Station on Goose Key, Mariana County. The jets on the flight line aren't locked.

Do you get a wanted level for taking a jet?

Yes. Taking off triggers an instant wanted level and jets will chase you.
