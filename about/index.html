About Vice Secret Story

Vice Secret Story is a fan-made guide to the secrets of GTA 6: hidden weapons, vehicles you can take early, easter eggs and trophies most players never see.

Every guide is written from the same short videos we post on Instagram and YouTube, with the location, the steps and what you get. If something works differently in your game, tell us and we'll fix the guide.
